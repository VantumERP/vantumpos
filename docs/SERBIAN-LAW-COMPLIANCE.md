## 1. Verdicts on the two headline questions

### 1a. ESIR-handoff legality — "may a non-certified back-office hand sales to a certified ESIR?"

**Verdict: defensibly YES on written sources, with one narrow recharacterization risk, no regulator statement either way, and hard operational constraints. The current pilot posture (companion back-office, shop double-rings on its own ESIR) is lawful today with no open questions.**

What written sources establish (all verified against primary texts):

- **Approval attaches per element, not to surrounding software.** An ESIR is the element "čija je upotreba odobrena od strane Poreske uprave, u koji obveznik fiskalizacije unosi podatke o prometu i iz kojeg se izdaje fiskalni račun" — [ZF čl. 2 st. 1 tač. 5](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji-republike-srbije.html) (definition is cumulative: data entry AND receipt issuance). The statute regulates only the obveznik, the dobavljač EFU, and the approved elements (ESIR + PFR, [ZF čl. 6 st. 1–2](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji-republike-srbije.html)); it nowhere defines or regulates software that prepares data upstream of an ESIR and issues no receipts. The public Registar records nothing about integrations, callers or data sources ([Uredba čl. 8, 11](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/uredba-o-obliku-i-sadrzaju-registra-elemenata-vrstama-elektronskih-fiskalnih-uredjaja.html); [live registry](https://www.purs.gov.rs/sr/eFiskalizacija/registar-odobrenih-elemenata-efu.html)).
- **Composite architectures are expressly contemplated.** The Uredba enumerates an ESIR "objedinjen u okviru softvera za planiranje resursa" (ERP-embedded) and contains an open clause for "drugi oblici" — [Uredba čl. 6 st. 1 tač. 2 i st. 2](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/uredba-o-obliku-i-sadrzaju-registra-elemenata-vrstama-elektronskih-fiskalnih-uredjaja.html).
- **PURS's own approval questionnaire contemplates externally-fed ESIRs.** File-based transaction input is a declarable optional ESIR feature ("P17: ESIR podržava unos podataka o transakciji preko fajla… — Opciono") and human operator input is likewise optional (P1 — Opciono) — [TU 7.4.2 tač. 10](https://purs.gov.rs/upload/media/2025/2/4/414144/Tehnickouputstvo-ESIRiliL-PFR.pdf). The prohibited-functions list (TU 7.4.2 tač. 9) contains exactly three items, all about not issuing/altering data without/from the PFR; **nothing prohibits an ESIR receiving sale data from external software** (verified by exhaustive read of the 54-page text).
- **The pattern is market-mainstream.** Softek ESIR Link is marketed as connecting "Vaše postojeće POS/ERP rešenje… bez potrebe za sertifikaciju vašeg softvera" (file-drop IN/OUT; Softek ESIR IB 338, approved 05.01.2022, never revoked) — [softek.rs](https://www.softek.rs/e-fiskalizacija/esir-link-za-programere/); Teron publishes a public REST API to its certified ESIR/LPFR — [api.teron.rs](https://api.teron.rs/). This is practical comfort, **not legal authority** — PURS approved the products, never blessed the abstract pattern.

The residual risk: a strict reading of [ZF čl. 2 st. 1 tač. 5](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji-republike-srbije.html) ("u koji obveznik unosi podatke o prometu") lets an inspector argue that where the sale is composed, priced and finalized in VantumPOS and the approved ESIR is a pass-through, VantumPOS is the de facto unapproved ESIR. Consequences — **penalty tier CORRECTED 31.07.2026, see [SW11-SW15-VERIFIED-RULES.md](SW11-SW15-VERIFIED-RULES.md) §1 row 4:** shop — prekršaj **50.000–500.000 RSD for a preduzetnik** ([ZF čl. 15 **st. 3**](https://www.purs.gov.rs/upload/media/2025/2/4/382229/Zakonofiskalizaciji.pdf)); the 300.000–2.000.000 figure is [ZF čl. 15 st. 1](https://www.purs.gov.rs/upload/media/2025/2/4/382229/Zakonofiskalizaciji.pdf), which opens *"kazniće se za prekršaj **pravno lice** - obveznik fiskalizacije"* and does not reach the pilot. The offence tačka (st. 1 tač. 3) is right. Separately, zabrana vršenja delatnosti **15 dana / 90 dana / jedne godine** per premises ([ZF čl. 12](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji-republike-srbije.html) — statute says "jedne godine", **not** "365 days") is an inspection measure available against any obveznik incl. a preduzetnik, but its **predicate is only failure to evidence each individual retail sale through the EFU (čl. 12 st. 1)** — it does **not** attach to a recharacterization or registry-check failure, so it is not a consequence of this risk. (A separate privremena zabrana for ignoring a rešenje sits in čl. 13 st. 4.) Actaer — dobavljač fine, fixed 300.000 RSD pravno lice / 150.000 preduzetnik ([ZF čl. 18 u vezi čl. 6 st. 6](https://propisi.net/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji/)) — verified correct. **No PURS FAQ answer or MF mišljenje exists on this question in either direction** (verified: exhaustive FAQ grep 16.07.2026 — the only ERP item concerns reprint-as-Kopija; budiefiskalizovan.gov.rs refused connections). This is precisely the gap to close with a written pitanje before scaling (§4, §6).

**Hard constraints that survive regardless:** every retail sale must be evidenced through the EFU with the fiscal receipt issued **at the moment of sale** ([ZF čl. 4 st. 2](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji-republike-srbije.html), [čl. 6 st. 1, čl. 8 st. 1](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji-republike-srbije.html)) — batch/end-of-day fiscalization is illegal; any future integration must be per-sale, through the target ESIR's **documented** input channel, after verifying its name+version+IB and absence of rešenje o ukidanju in the [Registar](https://www.purs.gov.rs/sr/eFiskalizacija/registar-odobrenih-elemenata-efu.html).

**Safe posture rules (binding on product and marketing until PURS answers):**
1. VantumPOS issues no receipts, prints nothing receipt-like, never talks to a PFR, never holds a bezbednosni element.
2. Pilot workflow is "ESIR first, VantumPOS second"; every sale rings the shop's certified ESIR at the till. Any request to "skip the ESIR for small sales" is refused in writing.
3. No output may resemble a fiscal receipt (no QR, no "FISKALNI RAČUN" framing, no PIB/PFR-number/brojač block); any future sale-itemizing print carries "OVO NIJE FISKALNI RAČUN" at ≥2× font, top and bottom ([PVFR čl. 2 st. 8–10](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/pravilnik-o-vrstama-fiskalnih-racuna-tipovima-transakcija-nacinima-placanja-pozivanju-na-broj.html); [TU deo 16 P10](https://purs.gov.rs/upload/media/2025/2/4/414144/Tehnickouputstvo-ESIRiliL-PFR.pdf)).
4. No feature usable for turnover suppression — this is the criminal red line: producing, selling or even **holding** software "koji služi za izbegavanje evidentiranja prometa, odnosno izbegavanje plaćanja poreza" is 1–5 years imprisonment + 1–5 year professional ban + confiscation ([ZPPPA čl. 175b, also 175a](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_poreskom_postupku_i_poreskoj_administraciji.html), in force 20.12.2022). Note "drži" criminalizes the pilot shop's possession too — the clean design protects both sides.
5. Marketing/contract language states expressly: "VantumPOS nije elektronski fiskalni uređaj niti ESIR i ne izdaje fiskalne račune; promet na malo se evidentira preko odobrenog ESIR-a prodavnice." Mere absence from the PURS register is NOT unlawful ([ZPPPA čl. 175a](http://www.parlament.gov.rs/upload/archive/files/lat/pdf/zakoni/13_saziv/2506-22%20lat..pdf) makes the evasion function, not non-registration, the constitutive element) — but the sentence removes the čl. 18 predicate.

Helpfully, an internal parallel sales/stock ledger is not merely tolerated — core elements are **mandatory** for the trader ([ZoT čl. 29–30](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html); KEP may be kept electronically, [PEP čl. 10](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/pravilnik_o_evidenciji_prometa.html)). VantumPOS's category is legally required software with no form or approval requirement.

### 1b. ZZPL — is Actaer a processor, and what must the DPA + software contain?

**Verdict: Actaer is NOT an obrađivač (processor) by default for a local-first app. It becomes one the moment its staff view or touch the shop's personal data on the shop's behalf — which describes AnyDesk support sessions. Recommendation: sign the čl. 45 DPA before the pilot regardless; it is cheap, matches Serbian vendor practice, and protects both parties.**

- **Default: not a processor.** ZZPL defines obrađivač as one who "obrađuje podatke o ličnosti u ime rukovaoca" and obrada as actions performed **with** personal data — delivering software the shop runs on its own machine, with no vendor access, is not obrada ([ZZPL čl. 4 st. 1 tač. 3, 9](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_zastiti_podataka_o_licnosti.html)). But obrada expressly includes **uvid** (viewing) and upotreba (use) — so an AnyDesk session in which Actaer staff can see users/sales/shift data IS processing on the shop's behalf. Hosted backups would be unambiguous processing ("pohranjivanje").
- **The line, per the standard interpretive aid:** [EDPB Guidelines 07/2020 para 83 + examples](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/documents/2023-10/EDPB_guidelines_202007_controllerprocessor_final_en.pdf) — an IT provider that "inevitably… systematically has access to personal data" is a processor ("a processor agreement is therefore concluded"); an "IT-consultant fixing a software bug" with "purely incidental… very limited" access is not, and the controller instead applies Art 32/čl. 50 security measures. Serbian vendor practice (Seyfor/Saop GTC) papers support-with-access as processor activity. Persuasive, not binding; the ZZPL provisions interpreted are near-verbatim GDPR transpositions (čl. 4, 45, 50).
- **DPA content is fully prescribed** by [ZZPL čl. 45](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_zastiti_podataka_o_licnosti.html) — the complete checklist is in §4. Correct stav numbering (verified): sub-processor flow-down + full liability = **st. 7**; processor-turned-controller = **st. 8**; Poverenik's standard contractual clauses power = st. 11 (basis of the SCC Odluka, Sl. glasnik RS 5/2020 — usable as a template). Statutory term is "vrsta lica", not "kategorije lica".
- **Software duties bite directly:** čl. 50 binds controller AND processor (tehničke, organizacione i **kadrovske** mere; **kriptozaštita named first**; instruction-only access st. 5); čl. 42 privacy-by-design/default is directly sanctionable — **tier CORRECTED 31.07.2026:** 50.000–2.000.000 RSD is [čl. 95 **st. 1** tač. 20](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_zastiti_podataka_o_licnosti.html), the *pravno lice* tier; a **preduzetnik faces 20.000–500.000 (čl. 95 st. 4)**. Encrypted backups additionally unlock the čl. 53 st. 3 tač. 1 notification safe-harbor for lost media.
- **Shop-side essentials:** lawful basis for employee data is contract + legal obligation ([čl. 12 st. 1 tač. 2–3](https://mduls.gov.rs/wp-content/uploads/Zakon-o-za%C5%A1titi-podataka-o-li%C4%8Dnosti.pdf)), NOT consent (restrictively construed in employment — build **no** consent UI); the shop keeps čl. 47 records despite its size because daily POS processing "nije povremena" (st. 9 tač. 2; **fine tier CORRECTED 31.07.2026** — the fixed 100.000 RSD of čl. 95 st. 2 tač. 5 binds a rukovalac *"koji ima svojstvo pravnog lica"*; a **preduzetnik faces fixed 50.000, čl. 95 st. 6**); employees get a čl. 23 notice naming Actaer as recipient during support. Breach chain: processor→controller bez nepotrebnog odlaganja (čl. 52 st. 3); controller→Poverenik ≤72h on the prescribed form ([Pravilnik 40/2019](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/pravilnik-o-obrascu-obavestavanja-poverenika-za-informacije-od-javnog-znacaja-o-povredi.html), scanned copy to povredapodataka@poverenik.rs); document every breach internally (čl. 52 st. 6–7); data subjects only on high risk (čl. 53). **No DPO required** for either party (čl. 56 st. 2) — do not voluntarily name one (triggers publish-and-notify duties); no database registration with the Poverenik exists under the 2018 law.

## 5. Certification path map — **NOT NOW; for a later decision**

If Actaer ever wants VantumPOS to issue fiscal receipts, the path is fully mapped and routine (~120 rešenja issued H1 2026; IBs ≈1.580). Decision inputs:

**Process** ([Uredba čl. 8, 11](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/uredba-o-obliku-i-sadrzaju-registra-elemenata-vrstama-elektronskih-fiskalnih-uredjaja.html); [TU odeljci 4, 7](https://purs.gov.rs/upload/media/2025/2/4/414144/Tehnickouputstvo-ESIRiliL-PFR.pdf)): (1) free registration at https://tap.sandbox.suf.purs.gov.rs/ (open to d.o.o. **and** preduzetnik; no commitment) → (2) e-mail confirmation → (3) PURS approves registration → (4) PFX certificates by e-mail unlock dev environment, build instructions, L-PFR auto-test tools, test bezbednosni elementi → (5) develop, file prijava za tehnički pregled with the full self-assessment questionnaire (documentation in Serbian) → (6) **tehnički pregled: iterative, NO statutory deadline** ("Potrebne tehničke izmene"/"Dostavljene tehničke izmene" round-trips) → (7) zahtev za odobrenje starts the **15-day** statutory clock → (8) administrativni pregled → (9) rešenje with IB + izveštaj o postupku odobravanja → (10) publication in the public register. Approval sought: **PRENOSIVO** (sold to shops), classification **OSNOVNI** (Promet/Kopija/Predračun/Obuka × Prodaja/Refundacija) or **NAPREDNI** (+ Avans) — TU 7.2.3 fixes the receipt matrix; part 17 requires sample receipts with readable QR (40×40–50×50 mm) incl. sa-ID-kupca variants.

**Deterministic engineering gap list vs today's code** (TU 7.4.2, all Obavezno): receipt printing stack — electronic AND printed, screen-only fails (deo 10 P10); hard block on issuing anything without PFR data (deo 9 P1 — the exact inverse of today's `not_fiscalized` flow); ESIR–PFR mutual authentication at startup (10 P2); tax rates exclusively from PFR/SUF, never local (deo 12 P1, P6); GTIN (10 P11); mandatory reference number for refunds/copies (10 P12); 30-day searchable electronic journal (10 P16); all 7 payment methods (Other/Cash/Card/Check/WireTransfer/Voucher/MobileMoney) plus the cash-consolidation mode switch — note PVFR čl. 6 st. 2's reduced set is **mandatory only for food/beverage-on-site and bakeries** (10 P7; split payment optional P8); item price rounding 2 decimals half-up (11 P4); item selection by name or GTIN scan (11 P5); product import/export (11 P6); Wi-Fi ESIR-to-PFR obavezno (deo 6 P1); "OVO NIJE FISKALNI RAČUN" ≥2× font on Kopija/Predračun/Obuka (deo 16 P10).

**Re-approval tax:** every change to an approved element requires a new zahtev ([Uredba čl. 9](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/uredba-o-obliku-i-sadrzaju-registra-elemenata-vrstama-elektronskih-fiskalnih-uredjaja.html)); PURS operationalizes as "svaka nova verzija koja utiče na izgled fiskalnog računa zahteva novo odobrenje" (TU 7.1), each version getting its own IB. **Architect the fiscal module as a small, isolated, version-pinned surface.** PURS performs field checks comparing deployed vs approved builds and can revoke by rešenje (halt sales + market withdrawal) — release management must guarantee shops run exactly the approved fiscal-module version (TU odeljak 6; Uredba čl. 10–11).

**Shop-side economics:** physical retail requires ≥1 L-PFR per poslovni prostor — V-PFR alone is legal only for pure internet / used-own-assets sales ([ZF čl. 6 st. 3–4](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-fiskalizaciji-republike-srbije.html); prekršaj čl. 15 st. 1 t. 4); V-PFR is free and PURS-hosted. Approved software L-PFRs cost ~€5/month (Master LPFR verified: 5€/mo or 50€/yr + PDV per location, ≤4 ESIRs; [mastersoftware.rs](https://www.mastersoftware.rs/sr/resenja/master-lpfr/)) plus a smart-card reader; the bezbednosni element is PURS-issued per premises to the **merchant** via ePorezi (Actaer cannot obtain it; renewal 1–4 years) ([Pravilnik o BE, 31/2021 i 96/2021](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/pravilnik-o-uslovima-i-proceduri-izdavanja-i-nacinu-koriscenja-bezbednosnog-elementa.html)). **No state fee for approval was found anywhere** (unconfirmed as a negative — §6.8). Realistic timeline for a near-compliant product: ~2–4 months sandbox-to-rešenje (2021 first wave ran 3–5 months); the unbounded variable is the tehnički pregled queue. A "partner with a certified cloud ESIR API" shortcut exists (e.g., efiskalizacija.cloud, IB 1522) but is legally awkward for in-store desktop retail — the checkout UI arguably becomes part of the element — so the clean options remain: stay non-fiscal (current) or certify an OSNOVNI/NAPREDNI prenosivo ESIR module.
