## 2. Scope verdicts

Four questions, answered first because each decides whether a roadmap item exists at all.

### 2a. Is SW-10 a LEGAL duty or a PRUDENTIAL control?

> **PRUDENTIAL / EVIDENTIAL. No Serbian provision requires a private retail controller to log access. Keep the feature — relabel it.**

Three independent legs, all negative:

**(i) Čl. 50 does not mention logging.** Its st. 2 lists candidate measures — pseudonimizacija i kriptozaštita; poverljivost, integritet, raspoloživost i otpornost; sposobnost vraćanja podataka; redovno testiranje — and **logging is not among them**. St. 5 is an *instruction-control* duty, not a record-keeping duty.

**(ii) The express logging duties bind only a nadležni organ.** Two of them, not one — the V1 finding claimed čl. 48 was the only one and that is wrong. Čl. 48 (belezenje radnji obrade) and **čl. 51 st. 2 t. 6) i 7)** (*kontrola prenosa* and *kontrola unosa*) both create after-the-fact traceability duties, and both are confined to *"nadležni organ koji obrađuje podatke u posebne svrhe"*. Čl. 50 st. 6 confirms the boundary from the other side: *"Odredbe st. 1. do 5. ovog člana ne primenjuju se na obradu koju vrše nadležni organi u posebne svrhe."* A boutique preduzetnik is not a nadležni organ and does not process for posebne svrhe (čl. 4 defines these as criminal-law purposes). **Expressio unius: the legislature demonstrably knew how to write a logging duty twice over, and confined both to competent authorities.**

**(iii) No fine attaches.** Čl. 50 is absent from čl. 95. So is čl. 51, and so is čl. 41.

**What this does NOT mean.** It does not mean "no consequence" — that is unverified (§6 R-9). The Poverenik's corrective powers under the nadzor chapter (opomena, nalog with a deadline) are the enforcement route against an unpenalised provision, and the register must say *"no prekršaj is prescribed"*, never *"no consequence"*.

**The honest citation for SW-10:** čl. 5 st. 2 + čl. 41 st. 1 (accountability — the controller must be *able to demonstrate*) and čl. 50 st. 1 (risk-proportionate measures), with **čl. 48 st. 2 and čl. 51 st. 2 t. 7 as the design template** borrowed from the law-enforcement regime. **Čl. 46 stays as an operative provision** — it is the penalised instruction-only rule and the real hook for remote support. **Čl. 42 stays too**, relabelled: it is the only penalised article in this cluster, and it is open-textured, so an audit log is one defensible way to evidence adequacy. Do not state, in either direction, that čl. 42 does or does not mandate logging.

### 2b. Does the SW-12 cenovnik duty bind a trader with no website — is our pilot in scope?

> **Split answer. SETTLED NEGATIVE: there is no "trader who has a website" carve-out in čl. 6 st. 2, and the project may not rely on one. UNRESOLVED POSITIVE: whether an inspector or prekršajni sud would require a website-less trader to create a site. SW-12 stays on the roadmap as a build item; the pilot must NOT be told it is in breach today.**

**Why the negative is settled.** The duty-bearer is *"Trgovac"* unqualified, and čl. 5 t. 2 expressly enumerates *preduzetnik*. There is no size, turnover, micro-trader or chain-only threshold anywhere in the statute (grep-verified). And the drafters used a conditional formula **three times inside čl. 6 itself** — st. 4, st. 5 and st. 6 each open *"Trgovac koji objavi cenovnik iz stava 2. ovog člana"* — and did not use it in st. 2 or st. 3.

**Why the positive is not.** Two of the V2 finding's three a-contrario pillars collapsed under refutation and must be struck from any argument the project makes:

| Pillar as pleaded | What the text actually says |
|---|---|
| "čl. 172 **st. 2**: *na svojoj internet stranici ukoliko je imaju*" | The formula is **čl. 172 st. 5**. Actual st. 2: *"Ministarstvo sačinjava i vodi listu tela i javno je objavljuje."* And čl. 172 st. 5 binds ***"tela"*** — ADR mediators, defined by st. 3 as individual natural persons with a law degree. **It is not a trader-facing provision**, so it is weak evidence of the legislator's drafting habit for traders |
| "čl. 63 **st. 1**: *na prodajnom mestu i internet stranici (u slučaju daljinske trgovine)*" | The sentence is **čl. 63 st. 4**. Actual st. 1: *"Potrošač može da izjavi reklamaciju trgovcu radi ostvarivanja svojih prava iz člana 56. i člana 102. ovog zakona…"*. And the parenthetical qualifies **which channel** the notice goes on, not whether a website exists |
| "čl. 6 st. 4/5/6 use *Trgovac koji objavi*, st. 2 does not" | **CONFIRMED verbatim, three times.** This pillar survives — but it proves only that st. 2 is *not limited to publishers*. It does not prove a non-publisher must become one |

Against the positive step: čl. 6 st. 2 says *"na **svojoj** internet stranici"* — a possessive presupposing an existing site — and **no ZZP provision obliges any trader to have a website**. Extending a prekršaj to an unwritten duty to create one runs into lex certa (ZoP čl. 3). No bylaw, no ministarsko mišljenje, no inspectorate guidance and no case law resolves it, and two independent secondary sources note the no-website case is simply unaddressed. **The V2 finding declared this "SETTLED" in its verdict while conceding in its own open questions that it "is not settled by the text alone" — that internal contradiction is resolved here against the settled label.**

**Product consequence.** Build the hosted-endpoint capability, because a trader who wants to comply cannot without one. Document the exposure honestly. **Do not build a sales motion on "you are already illegal."**

### 2c. Does SW-16 popis bind a preduzetnik, and does the bylaw prescribe the popisna lista columns?

> **(i) YES, it binds — and a retail boutique preduzetnik has no escape route. (ii) NO statutory form exists for the popisna lista. SW-16 designs its own layout. But the *izveštaj o popisu* HAS prescribed content.**

**(i) The scope chain, all four links verified.** ZoRač čl. 4 st. 1 binds *"predUzetnike iz člana 2. tačka 3)"*, and čl. 2 t. 3 defines those as natural persons *"koja poslovne knjige vode po sistemu dvojnog knjigovodstva"*. So the popis regime reaches **only double-entry preduzetnici** — and a Novi Pazar boutique is necessarily one:

- It **cannot be a paušalac** — ZPDG čl. 40 st. 2 t. 2) excludes *"trgovine na veliko i trgovine na malo"*, and the st. 3 escape hatch covers only a kiosk, prikolica or *sličan montažni ili pokretni objekat*. A boutique shop is not one.
- It **cannot use prosto knjigovodstvo** — ZPDG čl. 43 st. 3 confines it to *preduzetnik poljoprivrednik* and *preduzetnik drugo lice*.
- Therefore ZPDG čl. 43 st. 2 applies: dvojno knjigovodstvo, and the full ZoRač + PoP 89/2020 regime.

**(ii) No obrazac.** The pravilnik runs čl. 1–16 and ends *"Број 110-00-263/2020-16 / У Београду, 22. јуна 2020. године / Министар, Синиша Мали, с.р."* — no prilog, no obrazac, no column list. Keyword scan of the full official text: zero occurrences of *kazn*, *prekršaj*, *privredni prestup*, *nadzor* — it creates no offence either.

**What IS prescribed, and must not be lost in the "free layout" answer:**

| Prescribed | Free |
|---|---|
| The **content of the izveštaj o popisu** (čl. 13 st. 1) — build it as a structured template with required fields, not free text | The **columns and layout of the popisna lista** |
| The **process**: plan rada (čl. 8 st. 1–2), blind count (čl. 8 st. 5), two signature events (čl. 8 st. 5 and čl. 9 st. 3), separate lists (čl. 10 st. 3–4, čl. 11 st. 1, čl. 12 st. 2, čl. 2 st. 5) | The **medium and print design** |
| The **deadlines** (čl. 13 st. 2, čl. 14 st. 2) | — |

**Boundary warning.** The "layout is free" answer is regime-specific. For *prosto knjigovodstvo*, Pravilnik 140/2004 čl. 15–16 **does** prescribe columns (vrsta robe, jedinica mere, količina, prodajna cena sa PDV, prodajna vrednost, ukalkulisani PDV, razlika u ceni, nabavna vrednost; and for a nivelacija additionally postojeća PC sa PDV, ukupna prodajna vrednost, nova PC sa PDV, ukupna vrednost po novoj ceni, ukupno povećanje split three ways) — but that regime is legally unavailable to a retail preduzetnik, so **do not build it** (§5).

### 2d. Is SW-20 a compliance duty on the RETAILER at all?

> **NO. Descope SW-20 from the compliance roadmap and re-file it in the product backlog. Nothing in Serbian law requires a POS to store fibre composition, care or size.**

Three independent reasons, each sufficient:

1. **Two of the three attributes have no legal driver.** Čl. 28, as substituted by 86/2019: *"Одредбе чл. 21–24. овог правилника престају да важе 1. јануара 2023. године."* That killed čl. 21 (care, SRPS EN ISO 3758), čl. 22 (shrinkage), čl. 23 (size) and čl. 24 (their placement).
2. **The surviving element binds the wrong party.** Only *sirovinski sastav* survives (čl. 16 st. 1), and its creation duty is the proizvođač's (čl. 15 st. 1) or the uvoznik's (čl. 15 st. 2). The retailer is a **distributer** — ZTZP čl. 3 t. 7: *"pravno lice ili preduzetnik registrovan u Republici Srbiji, koje je uključeno u lanac isporuke, a nije proizvođač ili uvoznik"* — with a verify-only duty.
3. **Every operative rule regulates a physical artefact** — the tag on the garment, the catalogue, the commercial documentation, the packaging (čl. 16 st. 2), or the deklaracija displayed on/next to the goods (ZoT čl. 34 st. 4). The fiscalization regime requires *naziv artikla*, not composition.

**Three things the descoping must NOT take with it:**

- **Size and colour variant handling stays**, re-justified as merchandising/inventory functionality. A clothing boutique genuinely needs a variant matrix, per-size stock and per-size reorder. Do not let a compliance descope kill a real product requirement.
- **One conditional trigger arms if e-commerce is ever built.** Čl. 16 st. 3 requires the composition to be *"нужно уочљив за потрошача пре куповине, укључујући и куповину путем електронских медија"*, and ZoT čl. 34 st. 5 independently requires distance traders to display declaration data before purchase. Then fibre composition **in Serbian** (čl. 16 st. 7) becomes a real data requirement with a real offence attached.
- **One fact question flips the whole answer.** If the pilot imports directly from Turkey on its own name it is an **uvoznik** under čl. 15 st. 2, inherits the label-and-guarantee-accuracy duty, and genuinely needs per-style composition data for label generation. **Ask the shop** (§6 R-11).

**Pre-empt the objection you will get.** Čl. 1 (Predmet) **still recites** care, shrinkage and size as the pravilnik's subject matter. That is residual drafting debris in the consolidated text — a Predmet article cannot revive operative articles čl. 28 has repealed — but anyone spot-checking čl. 1 will conclude this memo is wrong. Note it in the register once. **The Serbian secondary literature on this topic is systematically stale**: consultant blogs, training blurbs and the Ministry's own 2014 *Vodič za primenu* all still describe čl. 21–23 as live, because they predate the sunset and were never revised. Only the consolidated čl. 28 controls.

### V1 — SW-10: the audit log

**Encodable rule.** No ZZPL provision obliges a private controller to record who accessed personal data. The log is built to discharge an **outcome** duty — *odgovornost za postupanje* (čl. 5 st. 2) and the čl. 41 st. 1 ability to *predočiti* compliance — and to evidence that every remote-support session had a **nalog** from the shop within the meaning of čl. 46. Its field list is modelled on the only two content specifications Serbian law contains, čl. 48 st. 2 and čl. 51 st. 2 t. 7, neither of which binds the client.

> **ZZPL čl. 50 st. 5** — "Rukovalac i obrađivač dužni su da preduzmu mere u cilju obezbeđivanja da svako fizičko lice koje je ovlašćeno za pristup podacima o ličnosti od strane rukovaoca ili obrađivača, obrađuje ove podatke samo po nalogu rukovaoca ili ako je na to obavezano zakonom."
> **ZZPL čl. 50 st. 6** — "Odredbe st. 1. do 5. ovog člana ne primenjuju se na obradu koju vrše nadležni organi u posebne svrhe."

> **ZZPL čl. 42 st. 1 t. 2** (open-textured, and the *only* penalised hook here) — "obezbedi primenu neophodnih mehanizama zaštite u toku obrade, kako bi se ispunili uslovi za obradu propisani ovim zakonom i zaštitila prava i slobode lica na koja se podaci odnose"

> **ZZPL čl. 51 st. 2 t. 7** (the *better* template — actor + timestamp for **unos**, which čl. 48 st. 2 does not give) — "može naknadno proveriti, odnosno utvrditi koji su podaci o ličnosti uneti u sistem automatske obrade, od strane kog lica i kada su uneti ('kontrola unosa')"
> **ZZPL čl. 5 st. 2** — "Rukovalac je odgovoran za primenu odredaba stava 1. ovog člana i mora biti u mogućnosti da predoči njihovu primenu ('odgovornost za postupanje')."
> **ZZPL čl. 5 st. 1 t. 5** — "se čuvati u obliku koji omogućava identifikaciju lica samo u roku koji je neophodan za ostvarivanje svrhe obrade ('ograničenje čuvanja');"
> **ZZPL čl. 47 st. 7** (the retention CONTRAST — *trajno* governs the register, never the log) — "Evidencije iz st. 1, 3, 4. i 6. ovog člana vode se u pismenom obliku, što obuhvata i elektronski oblik i čuvaju se trajno."
> **ZZPL čl. 95 st. 1 t. 23** — "se podaci obrađuju bez naloga ili suprotno nalogu rukovaoca (član 46);"

**Binds whom.** Čl. 50 st. 5 binds **both** *"rukovalac i obrađivač"* — the boutique as controller of its customer and employee data, and Actaer as processor during remote support — but binds them to an outcome enforced by nothing in čl. 95. **Čl. 42 binds the rukovalac only**; Actaer's čl. 42 exposure is contractual, via čl. 45 st. 4 t. 3 (*"preduzme sve potrebne mere u skladu sa članom 50. ovog zakona"*) and t. 6 (*"pomaže rukovaocu u ispunjavanju obaveza iz člana 50. i čl. 52. do 55."*), not statutory. **Čl. 46 reaches furthest** — the obrađivač *and* any *"drugo lice … ovlašćeno za pristup"*, i.e. the individual support engineer and the shop's own 2–3 cashiers.

**Penalties.** ZZPL prescribes **prekršaje only** — the whole penal chapter is the single article čl. 95, and the word *privredni prestup* appears zero times. So ZPP čl. 6 st. 1 does not bite and a preduzetnik is expressly tiered.

**Can a preduzetnik be the subject? YES**, of čl. 95 st. 1 offences under **st. 4** and of čl. 95 st. 2 offences under **st. 6**, personally and once. **There is no "odgovorno lice kod preduzetnika" tier** — st. 5 and st. 7 are expressly tied to a *pravno lice*, a državni organ, an AT/JLS organ, or a foreign legal person's branch. **Suppress those lines entirely for preduzetnik tenants.** A shop employee is unreachable under st. 4/st. 5 for these offences; the only employee-facing prekršaj is čl. 95 st. 3 (5.000–150.000, professional secrecy under čl. 57 st. 7 / čl. 76), a different biće.

So the čl. 47 evidencija failure (fixed 50.000) can be issued **on the spot during inspection**; the čl. 42/čl. 46 range cannot, and goes to the prekršajni sud.

**The čl. 47 dependency is a real, separate, penalised duty.** The sub-250 exemption in čl. 47 st. 9 is disapplied where *"obrada nije povremena"* — continuous POS, loyalty and payroll processing is not occasional. Treat the boutique as obliged, and generate the register (§4).

### V2 — SW-12: the machine-readable cenovnik

**Encodable rule.** ZZP ("Sl. glasnik RS" 35/2026, published 23.04.2026, in force 01.05.2026) čl. 6 imposes a seven-stav cascade on every *trgovac*, preduzetnik included: display prodajna and jedinična cena (st. 1); **publish on its own website, separately for each prodajni objekat**, a cenovnik in digital form suitable for automatic processing, and in doing so comply with st. 1 (st. 2); **update it in real time** to match current prices in the outlets and/or in distance selling (st. 3); adhere to the published prices (st. 4); **enable** software tools and automated programs to collect price data, in a way permitting comparison of previously published against real-time prices (st. 5); open an account on the Nacionalni portal otvorenih podataka and update the cenovnik there in machine-readable format **on every price change, in accordance with a standard prescribed by a bylaw** (st. 6); the Minister prescribes conditions, content and manner of publication (st. 7).

**Encodable rule.** Three retention regimes run in parallel over one terminated employee, and they do **not** conflict, because ZZPL storage limitation is relative to purpose and čl. 30 st. 5 t. 2 disapplies the erasure right where processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation.

**Why the documentation-only tier is unresolved.** The biće in t. 24 is described as *"ne obavesti Poverenika o povredi bezbednosti podataka"* — a failure to **notify**. The commercial framing is correct even though the doctrinal question is open.

**Correction to a tempting shortcut.** Do **not** encode "čl. 95 st. 5 reaches only an odgovorno lice u pravnom licu." **Can a preduzetnik be the subject? Yes — but only of the čl. 58 prekršaj.** Čl. 57 is a *privredni prestup* and ZPP čl. 6 st. 1 confines that to a pravno lice and its odgovorno lice. **Reading 100.000–3.000.000 off čl. 57 and applying it to the pilot would be wrong by roughly a factor of six at the top end.** The 100.000–500.000 band sits inside the ZoP čl. 39 st. 1 t. 3) preduzetnik range (10.000–500.000), with the maximum exactly at the ceiling — no conflict, nothing to quarantine. **Pravilnik 89/2020 contains no kaznene odredbe of its own**; a bylaw breach (wrong commission composition, unsigned/unprinted liste, late izveštaj) is sanctioned only derivatively, on the argument that the popis was not conducted *"u skladu sa ovim zakonom"*. **One certainty-marker correction to the V5 finding.** It marked the *shodna primena* of čl. 5 st. 1 (goods-handler exclusion) to the single person as UNRESOLVED, while treating čl. 8 st. 5 (blind count) as a hard unqualified constraint on that same person. **Both reach the single person through the identical čl. 6 st. 2 gateway.** They must carry the same marker. The product conclusion is unchanged — build the blind count as the default — but the register must not claim one is settled and the other is not.

## 5. What must NOT be built

1. **Never tell a client that ZZPL requires an audit log.** Čl. 50 appears nowhere in čl. 95. Overstating this is a credibility risk the moment their lawyer checks — and the register would be asserting a duty the statute does not create.
2. **Never say "no consequence" for a čl. 50 breach.** *"No fine"* is verified; *"no consequence"* is not. The Poverenik's corrective powers are the live route (§6 R-9). Say only: **"ZZPL ne propisuje prekršaj za povredu člana 50."**
3. **No employee-activity or productivity dashboards on top of the audit log**, and no per-cashier aggregates in the owner UI. In a 2–3 employee shop the log identifies each cashier by construction, so reuse for performance monitoring is a **new purpose** under čl. 5 st. 1 t. 2 needing its own basis and its own čl. 23 notice — and it walks straight into the SW-14 §6 W-4 *sistem za praćenje rada* gate (60 + 45 days).
4. **No search query strings, no before/after PII payloads, no JMBG/PAN/address/phone, no free-text notes in the audit log.** Requirement 4 is a prohibition, not a preference.
5. **No owner-editable or owner-deletable audit log.** Its whole value is that a Poverenik or a court believes it.
6. **Never copy čl. 47 st. 7's *"čuvaju se trajno"* onto the audit log or the breach log.** That phrase governs the register of processing activities. Applying it elsewhere puts the product in permanent breach of storage limitation.
7. **Never quote 200.000 RSD at a preduzetnik for the cenovnik.** It is čl. 210 st. 1, *pravno lice*. The pilot's number is **100.000 (st. 3)**.
8. **Never sell SW-12 on a "you are already illegal" premise.** The website-creation question is UNRESOLVED (§2b) and the čl. 210 fine only switched on at the general application date.
9. **Never put the cenovnik behind a WAF bot-fight rule, CAPTCHA, login, JS-rendering requirement or `robots.txt` disallow.** Čl. 6 st. 5 is a positive duty to enable; we would be manufacturing the breach.
10. **Never publish a cenovnik without `jedinična cena` and `jedinica mere`.** Machine-readable and non-compliant is still non-compliant.
11. **Never claim ZEOR requires ledger or shift attribution.** It does not. Cite ZoRač čl. 8 st. 4 + čl. 28.
12. **Never put a ZEOR fine figure in front of a customer.** Čl. 50 st. 1's 500.000–1.000.000 is the pravno-lice tier and does not reach the pilot; čl. 51's 300.000–500.000 names *"fizičko lice koje ima zaposlene"* and **doubles the ZoP čl. 39 st. 1 t. 1) ceiling for that class**. Say only that a separate statute imposes permanent retention of the personnel record and that the figure is unsettled (§6 R-1). **Keep this identical to SW-14 §5 item 16.**
13. **Never attribute ZoRač čl. 57's 100.000–3.000.000 to a preduzetnik.** It is a *privredni prestup* and ZPP čl. 6 st. 1 makes a preduzetnik incapable of committing one. The preduzetnik tier is čl. 58, 100.000–500.000.
14. **No "Delete employee" affordance, and no `ON DELETE CASCADE` pointing at the personnel table.**
15. **Never denormalise the employee name or JMBG onto transaction rows.**
16. **Never claim the fiscal receipt must identify the cashier.** Zakon o fiskalizaciji čl. 5 st. 2 enumerates 16 elements and none is a kasir or operater; the Pravilnik adds none. **And never import the FBiH *"kod operatera"* rule (Zakon o fiskalizaciji transakcija čl. 18(2)) — that is Bosnian law.**
17. **Never show book quantities on the popis count screen.** PoP čl. 8 st. 5. The current §3 SW-16 spec mandates exactly this and must be rewritten.
18. **No statutory-form renderer for the popisna lista, and no carry-over of the SW-9c KEP print-fidelity constraints.** No obrazac exists.
19. **Never label a popis output a *"propisani obrazac"*.** Available copy: *"Popisna lista — Pravilnik 89/2020. Pravilnik ne propisuje obrazac."*
20. **No prosto-knjigovodstvo popis variant for the retail vertical.** That regime is legally unavailable to a retail preduzetnik (ZPDG čl. 43 st. 3). If VantumPOS ever targets *preduzetnik poljoprivrednik* / *drugo lice*, Pravilnik 140/2004 čl. 15–16 **does** prescribe columns and a next-day PK-1 entry rule — a different module.
21. **No edit or delete of a posted popis.** ZoRač čl. 8 st. 4, penalised for a preduzetnik at 100.000–500.000 (čl. 58).
22. **Never risk-gate the breach record.** Čl. 52 st. 6 says *"svaku povredu"*.
23. **Never invent a breach-notification form, and never build a Poverenik submission API.** Use the Pravilnik 40/2019 obrazac; filing is *u pisanom obliku, neposredno ili putem pošte*, with a scanned copy to `povredapodataka@poverenik.rs` as the alternative.
24. **Never print a fine for a documentation-only breach-log failure.** UNRESOLVED under ZoP čl. 3 (§6 R-2).
25. **Never encode "čl. 95 st. 5 reaches only an odgovorno lice u pravnom licu."** It has a standalone *fizičko lice* limb.
26. **Never treat čl. 1 (Predmet) of the textile pravilnik as reviving čl. 21–24.** Residual drafting debris; čl. 28 controls.
27. **Never present SRPS EN ISO 3758 care symbols or SRPS EN 13402 sizing as legally required in Serbia.** Voluntary since 1.1.2023.
28. **Do not let the SW-20 descoping kill size/colour variant support.** It is a real merchandising requirement for a clothing boutique — just not a legal one.
29. **Never present the Ministry's 2014 textile *Vodič za primenu*, or any Serbian consultant blog on textile labelling, as current.** They all predate the 1.1.2023 sunset and were never revised. **Treat any search snippet describing čl. 21–23 as live obligations as contaminated.**
30. **No headcount gate and no plan-tier upsell** on the audit log, the breach log or the čl. 47 register generator. There is no small-controller exemption for čl. 5, čl. 30, čl. 42, čl. 46 or čl. 50, and the only size-based relief — čl. 47 st. 9 — is switched off where *"obrada nije povremena"*.

## 7. Source-hygiene note for the next verification pass

**Provenance defect recorded against V1, because it will otherwise be inherited.** That pass asserted its ZZPL text came from the MDULS ministry PDF, *"extracted locally and verified article-by-article"*, and *"independently cross-checked against Paragraf's consolidation — the two matched word for word."* Neither claim held: its scratchpad `mduls_zzpl.pdf` was a 46 KB HTML error page beginning `<html class="error">` with zero PDF page objects, and its two supposedly independent text files were **byte-identical** (same md5), both being a *Cekos In Ekspert* rendering. **The quotes themselves were independently re-verified and are accurate**, and later passes (V3, V4) did successfully extract the genuine MDULS PDF with pypdf — so the URL is sound and the substance stands. **What must not be inherited is the confidence rationale.** When a pass claims a two-source match, the next pass should check the hashes.

**A second, related lesson: do not trust a summarizer over raw text.** Three separate false leads in this batch came from summarization layers, not from the law — a WebFetch summary that returned ZZPL čl. 52's documentation duty as *"stav 5"* while simultaneously reproducing the phrase *"iz stava 6"*; Paragraf's own AI summariser asserting that *"the document does not contain a stav 12 in član 34"* when the raw HTML has exactly twelve; and Paragraf's `Objavljeno:` page metadata being mistaken for a gazette date, which nearly moved the shipped `CUTOVER_DATE` by five days. **Strip the HTML, count the stavovi, read the chapeau.**

**Texts read from primary or official sources in this batch:** ZZP 35/2026 from the National Assembly's own PDF of the adopted law (103 pp., pypdf-extracted) and cross-checked against Paragraf · Pravilnik 89/2020 and ZoRač 73/2019 from the Ministry of Finance's own DOCX files · the textile pravilnik from the Ministry of Economy's official consolidated PDF (94 pp., 21 amendment footnotes, all 86/2019) · the Poverenik's Obrazac and Kontrolna lista KL-001 downloaded live and unzipped · ZZPL from the promulgated text and a second consolidated rendering · ZEOR from Paragraf and the University of Belgrade Faculty of Law text · ZoT, ZTZP, ZPDG, ZoP, ZPP and Zakon o standardizaciji from Paragraf consolidations.
