## 1. Verdict on the flagged claims

### (a) Seasonal sales at most twice per year — **CONFIRMED, but MIS-ATTRIBUTED and MIS-DATED**

**čl. 37 st. 8** (NOT „čl. 36–37"):

> „Sezonsko sniženje je prodaja robe po sniženoj ceni nakon proteka sezone i najviše dva puta godišnje. Sezonsko sniženje započinje u razdoblju između 25. decembra i 10. januara i 1. i 15. jula."
> — [paragraf consolidated](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html) · [neobilten consolidated (Cyrillic)](https://www.neobilten.com/zakon-o-trgovini-3/)

**This is NOT new law.** Identical text is in the original 52/2019 at čl. 37 st. 5, verbatim, in the official government PDF: „Сезонско снижење је продаја робе по сниженој цени након протека сезоне и највише два пута годишње…" — [zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs, 52/2019](https://zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs/Portals/0/Resources/Zakon_o-trgovini.pdf). 35/2026 only renumbered it (5→8). **Do not present this to the shopkeeper as a 2026 obligation.** It has bound them since 2019.

### (b) Start windows 25 Dec–10 Jan and 1–15 Jul — **CONFIRMED** (same stav, same verbatim, same 2019 provenance)

Two hard sub-rules for the engineer:
- The constraint binds the **START only** („započinje u razdoblju"). A sale starting 10 Jan lawfully runs to ~11 Mar. **Do not validate the end date against the window.**
- Applies to **sezonsko sniženje only**. Do NOT apply to rasprodaja, akcijska prodaja, or promotivna prodaja.

### (c) Max 60 days — **CORRECTED. The brief collapses TWO DIFFERENT RULES.**

There are two unrelated 60-day caps. Encoding them as one is a real defect.

**60-day cap #1 — sezonsko sniženje, čl. 37 st. 9 (OLD, was st. 6):**
> „Sezonsko sniženje iz stava 5. ovog člana može trajati najviše 60 dana po sezonskom sniženju."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html)

Per-instance, not per-year: 2 × 60 = 120 lawful days/year.

**⚠ DRAFTING DEFECT — do not resolve „stava 5" literally.** The text still says „iz stava 5", but st. 5 is now the progressive-discount rule; sezonsko sniženje is st. 8. In the original 52/2019 the reference was correct (sezonsko *was* st. 5) — the legislator inserted three stavovi and failed to harmonize. Verified against the pre-amendment official text ([52/2019 PDF](https://zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs/Portals/0/Resources/Zakon_o-trgovini.pdf): „Сезонско снижење из става 5. овог члана…" where st. 5 *was* sezonsko). No ispravka found. **Implement the purposive reading (cap attaches to st. 8); never quote st. 9's cross-reference in inspector- or user-facing text.**

**60-day cap #2 — promotivna prodaja, čl. 36 st. 9 (GENUINELY NEW in 35/2026):**
> „Promotivna prodaja je prodaja robe koja se prvi put uvodi u ponudu trgovca i koja može da se nudi po nižoj ceni od cene koja će biti formirana u redovnoj ponudi. **Promotivna prodaja može trajati najduže 60 dana.**"
> — amending act čl. 13: „U članu 36. stav 9. menja se i glasi:" ([amending act](https://www.paragraf.rs/izmene_i_dopune/230426-zakon-o-izmenama-i-dopunama-zakona-o-trgovini.html))

Original 52/2019 had **no cap** and read „koja se **stoga** nudi" — verified: „Промотивна продаја је продаја робе која се први пут уводи у понуду трговца и која се стога нуди по нижој цени…" ([52/2019 PDF](https://zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs/Portals/0/Resources/Zakon_o-trgovini.pdf)). The softening „stoga nudi" → „može da se nudi" is substantive: the lower price is now permissive, and **first-time introduction alone starts the 60-day clock**.

### (d) Bonus correction the brief got wrong — the trigger is NOT „oglašavanje"

**čl. 37 st. 2** triggers on **selling** at a reduced price and binds the **prodajno mesto**:
> „U slučaju prodaje robe/usluge sa sniženom cenom, trgovac, odnosno pružalac usluge je dužan da pored ponude prodajnog podsticaja, u skladu sa članom 36. stav 2. ovog zakona, na prodajnom mestu jasno istakne i sniženu i prethodnu cenu."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html); verbatim-identical in [52/2019](https://zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs/Portals/0/Resources/Zakon_o-trgovini.pdf)

Advertising is **čl. 38** — a separate regime, untouched by 35/2026, using a **different term** („ranija cena", not „prethodna cena") and a different test. Verified: zero occurrences of „oglaš" anywhere in čl. 37. **Do not reuse the čl. 37 validator for ad copy.**

### (e) Also correcting the brief: st. 2 is not new either

The brief says the prethodna-cena regime is „in force since 1 May 2026". Half-true. **The display duty (st. 2) has been in force since 2019.** What 35/2026 added is only the **definition** (st. 3–5). Amending čl. 14 inserts *„posle stava 2"* — st. 2 untouched, un-renumbered.

## 2. Prethodna cena — exact encodable algorithm

### 2.1 Trigger

Fire when a campaign of type ∈ {`rasprodaja`, `sezonsko_snizenje`, `akcijska_prodaja`} makes an article sellable below its regular price at a given `prodajno_mesto`. (čl. 37 st. 1 + st. 2)

`promotivna_prodaja` **never** triggers this. It is not one of the three types in čl. 37 st. 1; it lives in čl. 36 st. 9 and is defined against a **future** regular price. **Never route it through the prethodna cena validator.**

### 2.2 General rule — čl. 37 st. 3

> „Prethodna cena je najniža cena po kojoj je trgovac **nudio** robu tokom perioda od 30 dana pre početka sniženja cena, osim za lako kvarljivu robu i robu sa kratkim rokom trajanja."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html); inserted by [amending čl. 14](https://www.paragraf.rs/izmene_i_dopune/230426-zakon-o-izmenama-i-dopunama-zakona-o-trgovini.html)

```
prethodna_cena = MIN(offered_price)
  over [campaign.start_instant − 30 days, campaign.start_instant)
  scoped to (sku, prodajno_mesto)
```

Five non-negotiable properties:

1. **„nudio" = OFFERED, not sold.** Compute over the shelf-price timeline, not over receipts. An SKU with zero sales still has a prethodna cena. This forces an append-only offered-price log with validity intervals `(sku, prodajno_mesto, valid_from, valid_to, price)` — it cannot be derived from transactions.
2. **Anchor on `campaign.start`, not `now()`.** Snapshot at creation.
3. **Per prodajno mesto.** Basis: čl. 30 st. 2 — „Za trgovinu na prodajnom mestu vodi se evidencija za **svako prodajno mesto posebno**" ([paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html)). The same SKU can lawfully carry different prethodna cena in two shops of one trader. Key the log `(sku, prodajno_mesto)`. *(Note: čl. 37 itself never says "per store" — this rests on čl. 30 st. 2 + čl. 37 st. 2 „na prodajnom mestu" + čl. 38 st. 2 „na svakom prodajnom mestu". Low risk single-store; settle before multi-store.)*
4. **The MIN must include previous promo prices.** Never filter promotional periods out to "find the regular price". That optimisation would produce an unlawfully high anchor. (Textual basis: st. 3 says „najniža cena po kojoj je trgovac nudio robu" without qualification. EU Commission Notice 2021/C 526/02 §2.1 reads the parallel Art 6a rule the same way — *interpretive only, Serbia is not an EU member, [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:52021XC1229(06))*.)
5. **Perishable carve-out is a DEFINITION carve-out, not a display exemption.** See §2.6.

**Day counting — AMBIGUOUS.** ZoT does not specify calendar vs trading days, nor boundary inclusivity. **SAFE DEFAULT: calendar days, window `[start − 30d, start)` — half-open, excluding the start instant, including the full 30th day back.** A wider window can only lower the MIN, i.e. can only *understate* the discount. Never narrow it.

### 2.3 Items <30 days in assortment — čl. 37 st. 4

> „Prethodna cena za proizvode koji se nalaze u asortimanu trgovca kraće od 30 dana je najniža cena po kojoj je trgovac nudio robu u periodu **ne kraćem od 15 dana** do početka sniženja cena."
> — [neobilten](https://www.neobilten.com/zakon-o-trgovini-3/) · [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html)

**EXACT SEMANTICS — this is where the brief was wrong and where a naive implementation is most dangerous.**

- ✅ This defines a **reference WINDOW LENGTH FLOOR**. It is **NOT** a ban on discounting new items. There is no deontic language („ne sme"/„zabranjeno") anywhere in čl. 37 attaching to short-assortment goods — verified by targeted search.
- ❌ **DO NOT implement a hard block on discounting items in assortment <15 days.** The brief's framing invites this. Blocking a lawful sale is real commercial harm and the statute does not say it.

```
t = time_in_assortment(sku, prodajno_mesto)   # continuous-offering age

if t >= 30d:  window = 30d                     # st. 3
elif t >= 15d: window = t                      # st. 4: t satisfies "ne kraćem od 15 dana"
else:          # t < 15d — LAW IS SILENT/INCOHERENT
               WARN + require explicit, logged human decision
```

**The <15-day case is genuinely incoherent** — the law demands a window „ne kraćem od 15 dana" that cannot be observed for a 5-day-old item, and provides no fallback. **SAFE DEFAULT: do not compute. Do not block. Surface:** „Zakon ne propisuje jasno pravilo za robu u asortimanu kraću od 15 dana" + require the shopkeeper to enter a prethodna cena manually with a recorded justification. Note the tension: st. 2 still requires *a* prethodna cena be displayed, so the combined effect is that such a sniženje cannot be lawfully labelled with a computed anchor. Warn hard; do not silently pass and do not silently block.

**⚠ HIGHEST-RISK TRAP — „asortiman" and returning goods.** The naive rule *"<30 days of price history → use the 15-day window"* is **wrong** for restocked items and for **seasonal clothing returning to the shelf** — which is exactly the July inventory the Novi Pazar pilot will discount. Such goods are not new arrivals; they have prior history across the gap.

**SAFE DEFAULT:** distinguish *"SKU first ever offered at this prodajno mesto"* (→ st. 4 candidate) from *"SKU offered before, with a gap"* (→ st. 3, reaching back across the gap using full history). The log must therefore record offering **gaps explicitly** (validity intervals, not change events) and retain far beyond 30 days.

*Supporting (interpretive, non-binding): EU Commission Notice 2021/C 526/02 §4.2 — "Goods should be considered as having already been on the 'market' where the seller resumes the offer of the same goods after a period of interruption, e.g. after the goods were temporarily out of stock or in case of seasonal goods, such as winter/summer clothes." ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:52021XC1229(06)))* — **flagged as UNCONFIRMED for Serbia:** Serbian text says „u asortimanu trgovca", the EU text says „on the market". These are not obviously the same concept. The conservative reading (full history across the gap) cannot overstate a discount, so ship it — but it needs counsel sign-off.

### 2.4 Progressive reductions — čl. 37 st. 5

> „U slučaju kada se cena postupno i bez prekida smanjuje u okviru istog sniženja, prethodna cena je najniža cena koju je trgovac **primenjivao** tokom perioda od 30 dana pre početka sniženja."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html) · [neobilten](https://www.neobilten.com/zakon-o-trgovini-3/)

**OBLIGATION, not permission.** Cast as a definition („prethodna cena **je**…") — the trader has no election.

**CORRECTS THE BRIEF:** the anchor is **NOT** "the price at the first reduction". It is `MIN(price applied)` over the **30 days PRECEDING campaign start**. Different number, different displayed value.

```
on campaign.create:
    campaign.prethodna_cena_anchor = MIN(applied_price)
        over [campaign.start − 30d, campaign.start)
    PERSIST IT.   # snapshot once — immutable
on each markdown step:
    use campaign.prethodna_cena_anchor    # DO NOT recompute
```

**Recomputing lazily at each step-down is the exact manipulation st. 5 exists to prevent.** Successive cuts would pollute the window, the displayed prethodna cena would drift downward, understating the discount — wrong in the shop's favour, i.e. actively unlawful.

**Verb divergence — do not collapse.** st. 3 says „nudio" (offered); st. 5 says „primenjivao" (applied). Arguably a narrower set. **SAFE DEFAULT: model both fields separately.** For st. 5 anchor on prices actually applied at the till/shelf. Do not silently reuse the st. 3 dataset.

**Perishable carve-out is textually absent from st. 4 and st. 5.** Do NOT propagate it there without counsel sign-off.

### 2.5 What delimits one action from the next — **STATUTORY GAP**

The only delimiters in the text: „postupno i bez prekida" + „u okviru istog sniženja" (st. 5). **The statute nowhere defines „prekid" or any gap length.** Verified: „prekid" appears exactly twice in the whole law — čl. 37 st. 5, and an unrelated working-hours provision.

**Real delimitation actually comes from elsewhere — čl. 36 st. 2 tač. 3:**
> „period važenja podsticaja, sa naznakom datuma početka i datuma isteka, a u slučaju rasprodaje sa naznakom „dok traju zalihe""
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html); verbatim-identical in [52/2019](https://zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs/Portals/0/Resources/Zakon_o-trgovini.pdf)

**❌ DO NOT implement automatic „prekid detection" or gap-based anchor reset.** The statute supplies no threshold; any number the software picks is invented, not law.

**❌ AND DO NOT implement „prekid → reset anchor to full price".** This is the single most dangerous inference available here. On a prekid, st. 5 simply stops applying and **st. 3 governs** — a rolling 30-day lookback that **includes the prior campaign's discounted prices**. So after an interruption the anchor **ratchets DOWN**, it does not reset upward. The opposite implementation produces illegal advertising and inverts the amendment's anti-evasion purpose.

**SAFE IMPLEMENTATION:**
1. Bind each campaign to its **declared** start/end date (čl. 36 st. 2 t. 3).
2. Campaign identity explicit and immutable (`campaign_id`); never auto-merge or auto-split by inference.
3. Record every return-to-regular-price event.
4. On any new campaign, recompute over the new 30-day lookback — which includes the prior campaign's lows. Never restore a pre-campaign price.
5. If a reset threshold is operationally needed, label it a **configurable business policy**, not a legal rule.

**Practical consequence to warn the shop about:** running the same akcija twice inside 30 days collapses the lawfully advertisable discount each round.

### 2.6 What must be displayed

**Default (čl. 37 st. 2):** BOTH snižena and prethodna cena, „jasno", at the prodajno mesto — **in addition to** („pored") the čl. 36 st. 2 offer block: vrsta podsticaja; precizno i jasno određenje robe/usluge; period važenja sa datumom početka i istekom (or „dok traju zalihe" for rasprodaja); posebni uslovi.

**Percentage is NOT required in the general case.**

**MANDATORY CARVE-OUT — čl. 37 st. 11 (was st. 8):**
> „Trgovac i pružalac usluge koji organizuju akcijsku prodaju sa rokom važenja do tri dana ne moraju da istaknu sniženu i prethodnu cenu, već jasno određenje procenta sniženja."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html)

**This is a SUBSTITUTION, not a free pass.** The „već" is adversative: the two-price duty is *replaced* by a *mandatory* duty to state the percentage. A ≤3-day akcija showing neither is unlawful. A validator encoding "may instead show a percentage" would pass a promotion displaying nothing.

Three further constraints on the ≤3-day branch:
- It keys on **„rok važenja"** = the **declared** duration at creation, not elapsed time. A 2-day akcija extended to 5 days retroactively breaches the two-price rule and must be blocked or re-labelled at extension.
- **It exempts DISPLAY only, never COMPUTATION.** čl. 37 st. 10 defines akcijska prodaja by reference to prethodna cena („po ceni koja je niža od prethodne cene te robe/usluge"), so the anchor must still be computed and the price must still be below it.
- The percentage itself is constrained by čl. 38 st. 2 (see §3).

**Perishable carve-out (čl. 37 st. 3) — SAFE DEFAULT and a genuine incoherence.** The exception „osim za lako kvarljivu robu i robu sa kratkim rokom trajanja" is attached to the **definition** in st. 3. **st. 2's duty to display a prethodna cena has no perishable exception.** So for perishables the law switches off the computation method but does not switch off the display duty, and supplies no substitute method. **DO NOT code "perishable ⇒ skip prethodna cena display".** That is an inference, not the text, and it is exactly the false assurance this project exists to prevent. Fall back to a merchant-entered value with recorded justification, and flag to counsel.

Neither „lako kvarljiva roba" nor „roba sa kratkim rokom trajanja" is defined anywhere in ZoT — verified by full-text search (two hits total: čl. 36's unrelated „pred istekom roka trajanja", and čl. 37 st. 3). **Per-article boolean, merchant-declared, default FALSE (stricter rule), with logged justification. Never auto-classify from category.**

### 2.7 Worked examples

Assume prodajno mesto NP-01, calendar days, window `[start − 30d, start)`.

**(a) Simple reduction — akcijska prodaja**

SKU `KOSULJA-M-42`, offered-price log at NP-01:

| valid_from | valid_to | price |
|---|---|---|
| 01.04.2026 | 09.06.2026 | 4.990,00 |
| 10.06.2026 | 14.06.2026 | 4.290,00 (akcija, 5 dana) |
| 15.06.2026 | 30.06.2026 | 4.990,00 |
| 01.07.2026 | (open) | 5.290,00 |

New akcijska prodaja declared: start **20.07.2026**, end 10.08.2026 (22 days ≤ 31 ✓ čl. 37 st. 10).

Window = `[20.06.2026, 20.07.2026)`. Offered prices in window: 4.990,00 (20–30.06) and 5.290,00 (01–19.07).

→ **prethodna cena = 4.990,00 RSD.**

Note the 4.290,00 from June fell *outside* the window — correct, the window is 30 days. Note also the 01.07 price *increase* to 5.290,00 does **not** become the anchor: MIN, not "the price before the cut". Snižena cena must be < 4.990,00.

Label renders: `5.290,00 → 3.990,00` ✗ **WRONG**. Correct: **snižena cena 3.990,00 / prethodna cena 4.990,00**, plus start 20.07.2026, end 10.08.2026.

⚠ Had the campaign started **12.07.2026** instead, the window `[12.06, 12.07)` would capture the 4.290,00 akcija days → **prethodna cena = 4.290,00**, collapsing the advertisable discount. This is the "successive campaigns ratchet down" effect. Warn the merchant *before* they schedule.

**(b) Progressive / multi-step — sezonsko sniženje**

SKU `JAKNA-Z-L`, offered/applied price at NP-01:

| period | applied price |
|---|---|
| 15.05 – 20.06.2026 | 12.900,00 |
| 21.06 – 30.06.2026 | 11.900,00 |
| 01.07.2026 → | 12.900,00 |

Sezonsko sniženje declared: start **05.07.2026** (∈ 1–15 Jul ✓ čl. 37 st. 8), end 02.09.2026 = 60 days ✓ čl. 37 st. 9, 1st of 2026 ✓, merchant attests „nakon proteka sezone".

Anchor window = `[05.06.2026, 05.07.2026)`. Applied prices in window: 12.900,00 (05–20.06), 11.900,00 (21–30.06), 12.900,00 (01–04.07).

→ **anchor = 11.900,00 RSD — SNAPSHOT AND FROZEN at campaign create.**

| step | date | snižena cena | prethodna cena displayed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 05.07 | 9.900,00 | **11.900,00** |
| 2 | 20.07 | 8.900,00 | **11.900,00** ← frozen |
| 3 | 05.08 | 7.500,00 | **11.900,00** ← frozen |

**Wrong implementations to guard against:**
- anchoring on 12.900,00 ("price at first reduction") → overstates discount, breaches st. 5
- recomputing at step 2 → window would include 9.900,00 → displays 9.900,00 → understates discount, breaches st. 5
- both are wrong; only the frozen 11.900,00 is right.

If the merchant returns the price to 12.900,00 on 25.07 and re-cuts on 01.08: that is a `prekid`. **Do not auto-detect it.** If the merchant explicitly ends campaign A and creates campaign B (start 01.08), campaign B's anchor = MIN(applied) over `[02.07, 01.08)` = **8.900,00** (campaign A's own low). It ratchets **down**, never back to 12.900,00.

**(c) Newly-introduced item**

*Case c1 — genuine first introduction:* SKU `PATIKE-NEW-44`, never offered at NP-01 before, enters assortment 01.07.2026 at 8.900,00.

→ Type = **`promotivna_prodaja`** (čl. 36 st. 9). No prethodna cena required, none computed, none displayed. Cap 60 days → latest end 29.08.2026. Must declare the future regular price and transition to it at expiry. Offer must still carry čl. 36 st. 2 particulars (start + end date).

*Case c2 — sniženje on an item 22 days in assortment:* SKU `MAJICA-S-38`, first offered at NP-01 on 25.06.2026 at 2.490,00, dropped to 2.290,00 on 08.07.2026. Sniženje (akcijska) declared start **17.07.2026**.

`t = 22 days` → 15 ≤ 22 < 30 → **window = 22 days = `[25.06, 17.07)`**.
Offered prices: 2.490,00 (25.06–07.07), 2.290,00 (08–16.07).
→ **prethodna cena = 2.290,00 RSD.** Snižena must be < 2.290,00.

*Case c3 — item 6 days in assortment:* first offered 11.07.2026, sniženje wanted 17.07.2026. `t = 6d < 15d`.
→ **No lawful window can be constructed. WARN + require manual entry + log the decision. Do not block. Do not silently compute over 6 days.**

*Case c4 — the trap:* SKU `JAKNA-Z-L` was offered Oct 2025–Feb 2026, off-shelf Mar–Jun, back on shelf 15.05.2026. `t` since return = 63 days → st. 3 anyway. But if it had returned on 05.07 (10 days before a sniženje), **do NOT apply st. 4** — it is not a new arrival. Use full history across the gap. See §2.3 trap.

### 2.8 Ambiguity register — SAFE DEFAULTS

| # | Ambiguity | SAFE DEFAULT (cannot overstate a discount) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 30/15-day boundary inclusivity, calendar vs trading days | Calendar; `[start − Nd, start)`. Widen, never narrow. |
| 2 | „nudio" (st. 3) vs „primenjivao" (st. 5) | Model both fields. Use each stav's own verb. |
| 3 | st. 4 „ne kraćem od 15 dana" for `t < 15d` | Warn + manual entry + log. Neither block nor compute. |
| 4 | „asortiman" — do restocked/returning seasonal goods get st. 4? | **No.** Use full history across the gap (st. 3). |
| 5 | „prekid" undefined | No auto-detection, no auto-reset. Explicit campaign identity only. |

## 3. Promotion/sniženje model requirements

### 3.1 Legally defined types — closed set of FOUR

**čl. 37 st. 1** exhaustively lists three forms of *prodaja sa sniženom cenom*:
> „Prodaja sa sniženom cenom može da bude: 1) rasprodaja; 2) sezonsko sniženje; 3) akcijska prodaja."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html)

Plus a fourth, separate concept in **čl. 36 st. 9**: promotivna prodaja.

```rust
enum CampaignType {
    Rasprodaja,          // čl. 37 st. 6–7
    SezonskoSnizenje,    // čl. 37 st. 8–9
    AkcijskaProdaja,     // čl. 37 st. 10–11
    PromotivnaProdaja,   // čl. 36 st. 9 — NOT a sniženje
}
```

A generic `is_discounted: bool` is legally insufficient — every downstream duty differs by type.

| type | duration cap | start window | annual limit | prethodna cena? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| rasprodaja | **none** | none | none | yes (čl. 37 st. 6: „po nižoj ceni u odnosu na prethodnu cenu") |
| sezonsko sniženje | ≤60d **per instance** (st. 9) | 25.12–10.01 or 01–15.07 (st. 8) | **2/yr** (st. 8) | yes |
| akcijska prodaja | **≤31d** (st. 10) | none | none | yes (display optional if ≤3d) |
| promotivna prodaja | **≤60d** (čl. 36 st. 9) | none | none | **NO** |

### 3.2 „Outlet", „Black Friday", „poslednji komad", „stok" — **NO SUCH LEGAL TYPES EXIST**

Verified by full-text search of both the official 52/2019 PDF and the current consolidated text, Latin **and** Cyrillic: `outlet`/`аутлет`, `Black Friday`, `crni petak`/`црни петак`, `poslednji komad`/`последњи комад`, `stok`/`сток` — **zero hits in all documents.**

**But absence ≠ unregulated.** čl. 36 st. 1 defines *prodajni podsticaj* **open-endedly** („…i to **naročito** sa sniženom cenom… odnosno **drugim pogodnostima**"). A "Black Friday" campaign is legally whatever čl. 37 st. 1 type it factually is — almost always akcijska prodaja, ≤31 days, full prethodna cena duty.

**Requirement:** permit these only as a free-text **marketing label** layered on a mandatory statutory type. Never a first-class type with its own rules. Never a selectable escape hatch from the price-history check.

Related traps:
- **„stok"** → the law mandates the literal phrase **„dok traju zalihe"** for rasprodaja (čl. 36 st. 2 t. 3). Wrong word for a mandated field.
- **„outlet"/damaged goods** → čl. 36 st. 3: „Ako se prodajni podsticaj nudi za robu kojoj je umanjena upotrebna vrednost (roba sa greškom, oštećenje, pred istekom roka trajanja i sl.), **taj razlog se mora jasno i nedvosmisleno istaći na prodajnom mestu**." Different obligation, not a reduction type.
- **„poslednji komad"** → čl. 38 st. 1 bans advertising incentives for goods held in obviously bait-level quantity unless the available quantity at start is stated.

### 3.3 Mandatory promotion record fields

From **čl. 36 st. 2** (verbatim §2.5 above) — applies to **all four types**, including promotivna prodaja:

| field | rule |
|---|---|
| `campaign_id` | immutable; explicit; never auto-merged/split |
| `vrsta_podsticaja` | mandatory (t. 1) |
| `scope` (precise SKU set) | mandatory (t. 2) |
| `prodajno_mesto` | mandatory — čl. 30 st. 2 |
| `datum_pocetka` | **mandatory, non-null** (t. 3) |
| `datum_isteka` | mandatory, non-null — **except rasprodaja**, where it is nullable and the tag renders the literal string „dok traju zalihe" (t. 3) |
| `posebni_uslovi` | free text (t. 4) |
| `prethodna_cena_anchor` | snapshotted at create, immutable per campaign (st. 3/4/5) |
| `razlog_umanjene_vrednosti` | required iff goods have reduced utility — čl. 36 st. 3 |
| `marketing_label` | optional free text, no legal effect |

Note: čl. 36 st. 2 says „naročito sadrži" — a **non-exhaustive minimum**. Presence of all four is not conclusive legal sufficiency; do not reject an offer merely for carrying additional mandated data (e.g. čl. 36 st. 4 voucher issuer/value).

### 3.4 Rasprodaja — two unique, mechanically-checkable duties

**čl. 37 st. 6:**
> „Rasprodaja robe je prodaja po nižoj ceni u odnosu na prethodnu cenu, u slučaju prestanka poslovanja trgovca, prestanka poslovanja u određenim objektima ili prestanka prodaje određene robe."

Closed list, no „naročito"/„i sl." — **exhaustive three grounds.** Rasprodaja is NOT a generic "big sale" and must not be a default. Force the user to select one of the three statutory grounds.

**čl. 37 st. 7:**
> „Trgovac je dužan da fizički izdvoji robu koja je predmet rasprodaje od prodaje robe pod redovnim uslovima. Od objavljivanja rasprodaje do kraja njenog trajanja trgovac ne sme da naručuje i uključuje u rasprodaju nove količine robe koja je predmet rasprodaje."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html) · [neobilten](https://www.neobilten.com/zakon-o-trgovini-3/)

**HIGH-VALUE CHECK VantumPOS can uniquely perform:** after a rasprodaja is announced, flag/block any `prijem robe` adding stock of an SKU in that rasprodaja. Directly detectable from inventory movements. Note the ban is **narrow**: „naručuje **i** uključuje **u rasprodaju** nove količine robe **koja je predmet rasprodaje**" — conjunctive, scoped to the rasprodaja goods. It is **not** a blanket purchasing freeze, and does not bar restocking other goods for regular sale. Window runs from **objavljivanje** (announcement), not from the price cut.

„Fizički izdvoji" is physical-world → attestation only.

### 3.5 Which validations are legally mandated vs merely helpful

**Legally mandated (the duty exists in statute; the software either enables it or doesn't):**
- display both prices / or % for ≤3d akcija — čl. 37 st. 2 + st. 11
- correct prethodna cena value — čl. 37 st. 3–5
- start+end dates on the offer — čl. 36 st. 2 t. 3
- durations: 60/31/60 — čl. 37 st. 9, st. 10, čl. 36 st. 9
- seasonal windows + 2/yr — čl. 37 st. 8
- rasprodaja grounds + no-restocking — čl. 37 st. 6–7
- reduced-utility reason displayed — čl. 36 st. 3

Note **two independent failures**, not one: (1) *is* a prethodna cena displayed (st. 2), and (2) is the displayed value *correct* (st. 3). VantumPOS can fail either separately.

**Helpful but NOT legally mandated (implement as warnings, never green checkmarks):**
- „zanemarljivo kratak period" anti-evasion detection (čl. 38 st. 4 — see §4)
- 1/5-assortment check for headline % (čl. 38 st. 2)
- "successive akcije will collapse your discount" warning

**Impossible to validate — attestation only:**
- „nakon proteka sezone" (čl. 37 st. 8) — **„sezona" is undefined anywhere in ZoT.** The software can check the two start windows mechanically but cannot verify the season elapsed. Do not pretend to.
- „fizički izdvoji" (čl. 37 st. 7)
- perishability classification

### 4.2 Enforcement mechanics — this raises the stakes on being RIGHT

Because čl. 67 prescribes **only a fixed fine**, ZoP čl. 168 st. 1 engages: „Prekršajni nalog se izdaje kada je za prekršaj zakonom ili drugim propisom od prekršajnih sankcija predviđena samo novčana kazna u fiksnom iznosu." And st. 2 **bars** the alternative: „Za prekršaje iz stava 1. ovog člana ne može se podneti zahtev za pokretanje prekršajnog postupka." ZoP čl. 168 st. 3: separate nalog per offender (hence 100.000 + 10.000 stacking for a d.o.o.). — [Zakon o prekršajima](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_prekrsajima.html)

**Consequence:** a bad prethodna cena is an **on-the-spot ticket at the inspection visit**. The evidence must be producible *at the visit*, not later in a court filing.

**Corrected nuance the research initially overstated:** liability does not attach on the spot. ZoP čl. 174 st. 1 gives 8 days to file a zahtev za sudsko odlučivanje; čl. 174 st. 6: once the court opens proceedings „smatra se da odluka o novčanoj kazni iz prekršajnog naloga nije izrečena". ZoP čl. 173 st. 1: paying half within 8 days accepts responsibility and extinguishes the rest; st. 2: silence for 8 days = acceptance by default, nalog „postaje konačan i izvršan". So: **same-visit ticket, 8-day window to pay half or force court review.** Do not put "just pay half" guidance in the product — that is legal advice VantumPOS is not positioned to give.

**The real operational consequence is the administrative measure — čl. 53:**
> st. 1: „Ako otkrije nezakonitost u poslovanju ili postupanju nadziranog subjekta, tržišni inspektor će zapisnikom o inspekcijskom nadzoru naložiti otklanjanje utvrđene nezakonitosti sa primerenim rokom za otklanjanje nezakonitosti."
> st. 2: „Rok iz stava 1. ovog člana ne može biti kraći od 24 sata, ni duži od dva meseca, **osim ako je za određenu nezakonitost ili povredu zakona ovim ili posebnim zakonom drugačije propisano**."
> st. 4: escalation to rešenje on non-compliance.
> st. 5: inspector may issue a rešenje **immediately, with no prior zapisnik and no rok**, incl. „radi sprečavanja poremećaja na tržištu".
> st. 7: „**Mera otklanjanja nezakonitosti ne isključuje istovremenu primenu drugih mera propisanih zakonom.**"
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html); identical in [52/2019](https://zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs/Portals/0/Resources/Zakon_o-trgovini.pdf)

**Requirement:** bulk-revert a promotion and reprint tags **within 24h**. A validator that blocks at creation but offers no rapid remediation path leaves the shop unable to meet an inspector's deadline. **Do not encode "min 24h" as a guaranteed grace period** (st. 2's carve-out + st. 5's immediate-rešenje power), and **do not tell shops the fine is the alternative to the measure** — st. 7 makes them cumulative.

### 4.3 The advertising hook — čl. 38 (separate offence, čl. 67 st. 1 tač. 9)

**čl. 38 st. 4** — 7 stavova, **untouched by 35/2026** (verified: čl. 38 appears nowhere in the amending act's operative text; the only occurrence of „članu 38" is the čl. 67 t. 9 cross-reference):
> „Zabranjeno je oglašavanje prodajnih podsticaja kojim se oglašava navodna rasprodaja ili prividno sniženje cene robe ili usluga, obim sniženja ili sl., ako je **ranija cena** neistinito prikazana ili je roba/usluga bila ponuđena po ranijoj ceni u **zanemarljivo kratkom periodu**."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html); verbatim in [52/2019](https://zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs/Portals/0/Resources/Zakon_o-trgovini.pdf)

**This is the anti-evasion backstop that survives independently of the čl. 37 arithmetic.** A sniženje can pass st. 3 perfectly and still breach čl. 38 st. 4 if the anchor price existed only for a token period.

⚠ Note the penalty citation čl. 67 st. 1 t. 9) is **NEW as of 35/2026** — pre-amendment it was čl. 68 st. 1 t. 11) at 500k–2M.

**čl. 38 st. 2** — machine-verifiable:
> „Kod oglašavanja procenta sniženja, najveći procenat sniženja može da se navede ukoliko se odnosi na najmanje jednu petinu robe u asortimanu trgovca na **svakom prodajnom mestu** na koji se prodajni podsticaj odnosi."

### 4.4 What evidence the trader must produce

**⚠ THE KEY ARCHITECTURAL FINDING: the KEP book can NEVER prove a prethodna cena.**

The statutory hook is **čl. 30 st. 1**: „Trgovac je dužan da vodi evidenciju prometa robe na osnovu isprava iz člana 29. ovog zakona"; **st. 2** per prodajno mesto; **st. 6** „…učini dostupnom **na prodajnom mestu**"; **st. 8** delegates to the minister. *(Numbering is post-35/2026 — amending čl. 8 inserted a new st. 5 and renumbered 5→6, 6→7, 7→8. Pre-amendment these were st. 5 and st. 7.)* — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html)

That bylaw is **Pravilnik o evidenciji prometa („Sl. glasnik RS", br. 99/2015 i 44/2018 – dr. zakon)** — [official PIS](https://pravno-informacioni-sistem.rs/eli/rep/sgrs/ministarstva/pravilnik/2015/99/1/reg) · [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/pravilnik_o_evidenciji_prometa.html). It survives under ZoT čl. 71 st. 2 („Do donošenja podzakonskih akata… primenjivaće se podzakonski akti doneti do dana stupanja na snagu ovog zakona, osim odredaba koje su u suprotnosti sa ovim zakonom").

**Pravilnik čl. 3 st. 2** — the KEP has exactly **five columns**: redni broj / datum / opis promene / iznos zaduženja / iznos razduženja. **čl. 15 st. 1**: „Obrazac Knjiga evidencije čini pet kolona označenih brojevima od 1 do 5."

**There is NO per-article price field.** The nivelacija entry (**čl. 15 st. 5 tač. 5** — *not* čl. 8, which is the fuel-pump report; and *not* st. 4, which governs kolona 3) is a **value-level** adjustment:
> „5) trgovac upisuje i podatke o: **promeni cene robe usled nivelacije**, promeni stope PDV, vraćanju, otpisu robe… **Evidentiranje u slučaju promene cene robe vrši se na osnovu popisa ili podataka iz poslovnih knjiga**… za iznos kojim se uvećava vrednost robe, vrši se evidentiranje kao i pri nabavci robe, a u slučaju **smanjenja vrednosti robe vrši se storniranje crvenim stornom**…"

It carries **no old/new price pair and no per-SKU timestamp**. Kolona 2 records only „datum evidentiranja (**dan i mesec**)" — no time, no year — and čl. 11 st. 1 permits booking „**najkasnije narednog dana za prethodni dan**". A KEP entry cannot reliably pin even the *day* of a price change.

**Conclusion: VantumPOS must retain TWO linked artifacts:**
1. **The statutory KEP value entry** + its verodostojna isprava (čl. 12 st. 1: „…zapisnik, popisna lista…"), linked by redni broj (čl. 15 st. 2: „Redni broj se upisuje na evidentiranoj ispravi… koje se odlažu po redosledu"). For a **nivelacija** the isprava is a **popis / popisna lista / podaci iz poslovnih knjiga** — **NOT a zapisnik** (zapisnik belongs to the otpis/manjak branch).
2. **A NEW per-SKU append-only OFFERED-price log** — `(sku, prodajno_mesto, valid_from, valid_to, price)`. No bylaw prescribes its format, so it is free-form; its only legal job is discharging the evidentiary burden under čl. 37/38. **Note: since st. 3 says „nudio", it cannot be derived from receipts either.**

**Do NOT tell the shop that keeping KEP properly satisfies čl. 37.** It does not and cannot.

A unit price on the supporting document, not an old/new pair. **čl. 15 st. 5 tač. 1** (kalkulacija) requires nabavna cena po jedinici mere, marža, prodajna cena po jedinici mere — the natural seed row per (sku, prodajno_mesto). **ZoT čl. 29 st. 5** (added by 35/2026 čl. 7) expressly blesses electronic form.

→ **Zakon o računovodstvu („Sl. glasnik RS", br. 73/2019 i 44/2021 – dr. zakon) čl. 28** — [APR official PDF](https://www.apr.gov.rs/upload/Portals/0/zakoni%20uredbe%20pravilnici/Zakoni/Zakon_o_racunovodstvu.pdf):
> st. 5: „Помоћне књиге чувају се **пет година, од дана њиховог закључивања**."
> st. 7: „**Пет година** се чувају исправе на основу којих се уносе подаци у пословне књиге."
> st. 9: „Рокови чувања… рачунају се **од последњег дана пословне године** на коју се односе."

**Anchor tension:** čl. 19 routes to the *pomoćne knjige* rok (st. 5, „od dana zaključivanja"), but st. 9 anchors to year-end. Closing can lag year-end. **SAFE DEFAULT: retain until the LATER of (a) 5y from zaključivanje of that year's KEP, (b) 5y from 31 Dec of that business year. Never auto-delete on the earlier date.** Largely collapses in practice because Pravilnik čl. 17 st. 1 fixes closing at the year boundary.

**⚠ NEVER prune price history on a rolling 30-day window** because the lookback is 30 days. That would destroy legally required evidence. Retention is ~5–6 years.

The **ZoT čl. 70 st. 1** prekršaj limitation is only 2 years („Prekršajni postupak za prekršaje iz čl. 67. i 68. ovog zakona ne može se pokrenuti niti voditi ako proteknu **dve godine** od dana kada je prekršaj učinjen") — **the longer accounting retention governs.** Do not let anyone argue records drop at 2 years.

### 4.6 Tamper-resistance + print — express regulatory requirements

**Pravilnik čl. 14 st. 1:** „Knjiga evidencije vodi se ažurno, uredno i tačno, **na način koji ne dozvoljava brisanje unetih podataka, po hronološkom redu**." (Continuous duty. st. 2: responsibility rests on računopolagač/preduzetnik — record who.)

**Pravilnik čl. 10 st. 3:** „Kada se Knjiga evidencije vodi elektronski, radi dostupnosti evidencije nadležnim kontrolnim organima, **na njihov zahtev, vrši se štampanje podataka**." (An obligation to *actually print* on request, not mere theoretical printability.)

**Pravilnik čl. 18:** „Knjiga evidencije koja se vodi elektronski **na kraju poslovne godine** štiti se na način da **nije moguća izmena listova ili delova evidencije** i da se **može u svakom trenutku odštampati**."

⚠ **„nije moguća izmena" = alteration must be IMPOSSIBLE (prevention), not merely detectable.** A hash chain / audit trail that only *reveals* edits does **not** satisfy čl. 18 on its face. The record must be locked.

**Pravilnik čl. 17 st. 4** (missing from most summaries): „Ako se evidencija vodi u elektronskom obliku **zaključivanje se vrši štampanjem početne strane i krajnjeg salda**, koji se **overava potpisom** računopolagača, odnosno preduzetnika."

This is the direct regulatory justification for the append-only local store + year-end seal, and it pairs with SW-2's backup encryption — **but must NOT make records unprintable.**

**čl. 69a (NEW, added by amending čl. 26):**
> „Kazna za prekršaje odmerava se u skladu sa zakonom kojim se uređuju prekršaji, uzimajući u obzir olakšavajuće i otežavajuće okolnosti, kao što su: 1) priroda povrede…; 3) **obim i trajanje** povrede…; 4) **sve radnje koje je trgovac preduzeo kako bi ublažio ili popravio štetu**…; 5) ranije… povrede trgovca; 6) finansijska dobit…"

Two-edged, and both edges matter:
- **Against us:** a systematic miscalculation baked into POS software across thousands of SKUs over months is an *aggravating* factor (t. 3, t. 6). **This is precisely why a wrong rule in VantumPOS is worse than no feature** — it converts a one-off mistake into a sustained, high-volume, quantifiable-gain violation.
- **For us:** a tamper-evident log showing fast detection and correction is legally relevant mitigation (t. 4).
- **Caveat:** čl. 67's amount is **fixed**, so čl. 69a has little room to operate there. **Do not claim in product copy that good records reduce a čl. 67 fine.** Its value is evidentiary.

## 5. What VantumPOS must build (and must NOT)

### 5.1 MUST BUILD

**Data model**
1. `CampaignType` closed enum {rasprodaja, sezonsko_snizenje, akcijska_prodaja, promotivna_prodaja}. Type drives every downstream duty.
2. Campaign entity with immutable `campaign_id`, mandatory `datum_pocetka`, `datum_isteka` (nullable **only** for rasprodaja → renders „dok traju zalihe"), `prodajno_mesto`, precise SKU scope, `vrsta_podsticaja`, `posebni_uslovi`, `prethodna_cena_anchor` (snapshot at create, immutable).
3. **Append-only offered-price log**, keyed `(sku, prodajno_mesto)`, storing **validity intervals** `(valid_from, valid_to, price)` — intervals, not change events, so **offering gaps are explicit** (needed for the returning-seasonal-goods trap, §2.3). No UPDATE, no DELETE — corrections by reversing entries, mirroring the pravilnik's own crveni-storno logic. Chronological. Year-end lock (Pravilnik čl. 14, čl. 18).
4. **Linked KEP nivelacija artifacts**: per-article nivelacioni record (isprava = popis/popisna lista/poslovne knjige) + the KEP value entry referencing it by redni broj. **Two artifacts, not one.**
5. Per-article `lako_kvarljiva` / `kratak_rok_trajanja` boolean — merchant-declared, **default FALSE**, with logged justification and author. Never auto-inferred from category.
6. Retention: 5 years from the LATER of KEP zaključivanje and 31 Dec of the business year. **Block/warn on any purge inside that window.**

**Computation** — exactly §2.2–§2.4. Snapshot-once anchors. Per prodajno mesto. Never filter promo prices out of the MIN. Never recompute mid-campaign.

**Validations (hard)**
- sezonsko: start ∈ [25.12–10.01] ∪ [01.07–15.07]; ≤2 per calendar year by start date; ≤60 days per instance
- akcijska: ≤31 days
- promotivna: ≤60 days; SKU must have **no prior price/sale history** at that prodajno mesto; must transition to a declared regular price at expiry
- rasprodaja: one of the three statutory grounds must be selected; flag any `prijem robe` adding stock of an in-rasprodaja SKU
- akcijska ≤3 days: **require EITHER (snižena + prethodna) OR a stated percentage — never neither.** Evaluate from **declared** duration; block or re-label at extension past 3 days.

**Warnings (soft, never green-checkmark)**
- anchor price was in effect only 1–2 days inside the window → čl. 38 st. 4 „zanemarljivo kratak period" risk
- repeated short akcije for the same SKU inside 30 days
- headline % covering <20% of assortment at that prodajno mesto (čl. 38 st. 2)
- price history truncated / doesn't cover the full 30 days → say so, don't silently compute

**Attestations (software cannot verify)**
- „nakon proteka sezone"
- „fizički izdvojena roba" for rasprodaja
- perishability classification

**Output**
- Label renderer branching on type + declared duration
- **Bulk-revert + reprint within 24h** (čl. 53 st. 2)
- **Print-on-demand + self-contained inspector export** (Pravilnik čl. 10 st. 3, čl. 18; ZoT čl. 30 st. 6 — available *at the prodajno mesto*, offline; ZoT čl. 48 st. 1 t. 2 — the inspector may take a copy, so the export must be walk-away-able and must not expose unrelated data)
- Report: "tags requiring prethodna cena correction" — for a store-wide akcija, only SKUs whose current tag price is not the 30-day MIN need reprinting. Real labour saving.
- Year-end close: **print početna strana + krajnji saldo for signature** (Pravilnik čl. 17 st. 4)

**Citation hygiene**
- Maintain a **date-keyed stav map**. Conduct before 01.05.2026 cites old numbering (sezonsko = st. 5/6); on/after cites st. 8/9. **A single hardcoded citation constant is a bug.**
- Cutover date as a **configurable constant**, not a literal.

### 5.3 Verified negative — no sniženje bylaw exists, and none can

**čl. 36 and čl. 37 contain NO delegation clause.** No „Ministar bliže propisuje" sentence exists in either article. The complete delegation set in the consolidated text: čl. 11a, čl. 14, čl. 16, čl. 26, čl. 30 st. 8, čl. 34, **čl. 35 st. 10**, čl. 40 (Vlada), čl. 44, čl. 47, čl. 59. None reaches sniženje, prodajni podsticaji, or prethodna cena.

The only isticanje-adjacent delegation is **čl. 35 st. 10** — textually confined to unit price:
> „Ministar bliže propisuje vrstu robe za koju se ističe jedinična cena i način isticanja."
> — [paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html); Cyrillic identical in [52/2019](https://zastitapotrosaca.gov.rs/Portals/0/Resources/Zakon_o-trgovini.pdf). (Amending čl. 12 edits only st. 1's wording and adds no stav, so čl. 35 is not renumbered.)

Legal basis for "cannot lawfully exist" (softened from the researcher's absolutist framing): **Zakon o državnoj upravi čl. 16** — „Ministarstva i posebne organizacije mogu donositi propise **samo kad su na to izričito ovlašćeni zakonom ili propisom Vlade**" + may not „ustanovljavati prava i obaveze koje nisu već ustanovljene zakonom" ([paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_drzavnoj_upravi.html)). That permits authorization by *zakon OR Vlada regulation* — but ZoT delegates nothing on sniženje to the Vlada either, leaving no route.

**Consequence: the statute text of čl. 37 IS the complete specification.** Do not design around a forthcoming pravilnik. (Caveat: the Pravilnik o evidenciji prometa *is* a live bylaw under čl. 30 st. 8, and a replacement could change the KEP record format — see §6.)

## 6. Still unverified

### 6.1 čl. 37 st. 4 — the <15-day case (HIGHEST RISK)

**Tried:** full-text search of čl. 37 for prohibitory language („ne sme"/„ne može"/„zabranjeno") — zero hits attaching to short-assortment goods. Bill text + obrazloženje ties it to Dir. 98/6/EZ art. 6a(3)/(4). No pravilnik, no Ministry opinion located.
**Question:** *Za robu u asortimanu kraće od 15 dana, čl. 37 st. 4 ne daje primenljiv referentni period. Da li trgovac (a) ne sme organizovati sniženje, (b) sme uz prethodnu cenu utvrđenu na osnovu raspoloživog kraćeg perioda, ili (c) sme bez isticanja prethodne cene? Kako se to miri sa obavezom iz čl. 37 st. 2?*
**Risk:** blocking → lost lawful promotions (commercial harm); computing → possible unlawful advertising + a čl. 67 t. 8 prekršaj. **Our default (warn + manual entry + log) is deliberately the only option that does neither.** Note the two research passes *disagreed* here — one recommended hard-block, the other warn-only. Do not ship a block without counsel.

### 6.2 „Asortiman" — returning seasonal / restocked goods

**Tried:** Serbian text says „u asortimanu trgovca"; EU Commission Notice 2021/C 526/02 §4.2 says "on the market" and excludes returning seasonal goods from the derogation. **These are not obviously the same concept**, and the Notice is non-binding in Serbia (its own disclaimer: "This Notice is intended purely as a guidance document").
**Question:** *Da li „u asortimanu trgovca kraće od 30 dana" (čl. 37 st. 4) obuhvata robu koja se vraća na policu posle prekida (sezonska odeća, roba koja je bila rasprodata)? Ili se period računa od prvog ikad uvođenja u asortiman na tom prodajnom mestu?*
**Risk:** directly hits the pilot's 1–15 Jul window. Wrong answer → 15-day window on returning summer stock → anchor too high → overstated discount → čl. 38 st. 4 exposure. Ship the conservative reading; get sign-off.

### 6.3 „Lako kvarljiva roba" / „roba sa kratkim rokom trajanja"

**Risk:** abuse vector with no statutory answer. Our default (no auto-detection, explicit campaign identity, anchor never resets upward) closes the evasion path without inventing law.

### 6.5 „Sezona" undefined + winter-window year boundary

**Tried:** ZoT specifies nothing. Whether general time-computation rules apply in a prekršaj context is unconfirmed.
**Question:** *Da li se „30 dana" / „15 dana" računaju kao kalendarski dani i da li je dan početka sniženja uključen?*
